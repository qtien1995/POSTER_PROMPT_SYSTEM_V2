## 1. Deep 6-Layer DNA Reverse-Engineering Framework

When analyzing any reference image, extract these 6 foundational layers:

### Layer 1: Composition Geometry & Spatial Grid
- **Grid Layout:** Rule-of-thirds, symmetrical center, dynamic diagonal, or modular bento.
- **Visual Weight & Eye Path:** Focal entry point → Secondary scan path → Anchor point.
- **Negative Space Ratio:** Percentage of frame reserved for breathing room and typography (typically 35–45%).
- **Depth Staging:** Foreground framing elements, sharp midground hero subject, atmospheric deep background.

### Layer 2: Lighting Physics & Volumetrics
- **Key Light:** Direction (e.g. 45-degree top-left), softness, falloff rate.
- **Fill & Ambient Bounce:** Shadow fill ratio (e.g. 3:1 ratio for dramatic mood or 1.5:1 for bright commercial feel).
- **Rim / Edge Light:** Backlight intensity separating the hero subject from the dark background.
- **Color Temperature & Atmosphere:** Kelvin rating (e.g. warm 3200K gold hour vs cool 6500K clinical), volumetric haze, atmospheric dust motes or clean studio air.

### Layer 3: Color Science & Palette Grading
- **Dominant Base (60%):** Primary background/environmental tone (e.g. Deep Manulife Pine Green `#0B3B24` or Warm Ivory `#F8F6F0`).
- **Secondary Harmonic (30%):** Supporting structural tone (e.g. Emerald `#1B6B48` or Charcoal `#222222`).
- **Micro Accent (10%):** High-contrast pop for focal elements & badges (e.g. Champagne Gold `#E5C378` or Luminous Mint `#00D084`).
- **Tonal Contrast:** Shadow tint (e.g. teal-shifted shadows) and highlight rolloff.

### Layer 4: Materiality, Shaders & Surface Textures
- **Physical Surfaces:** Brushed anodized titanium, polished Carrara marble, warm walnut wood grain, frosted translucent acrylic/glass, emerald lacquer, uncoated heavy matte cotton cardstock.
- **Subsurface Scattering (SSS):** Organic translucency on human skin, foliage, or translucent marble.
- **Surface Imperfections:** Subtle micro-grain, realistic specular reflections, natural tactile textures (anti-plastic look).

### Layer 5: Camera Optics & Lens Characteristics
- **Focal Length:** 35mm (wide contextual editorial), 50mm (natural eye perspective), 85mm–105mm (compressed telephoto portrait / macro luxury product).
- **Aperture & Depth of Field:** f/1.8 – f/2.8 (silky bokeh background blur) vs f/8 – f/11 (edge-to-edge tack-sharp infographic architecture).
- **Camera Angle:** Heroic slight low-angle, confident eye-level, or top-down flat-lay.

### Layer 6: Integrated Graphic Design & Typography Architecture (ChatGPT Images 2.5 Standard)
Posters created by this engine are **not raw photos with pasted text**; they are complete **graphic design compositions**:
- **Multi-Font Pairing Hierarchy (Anti-Monotony Rule):** Bắt buộc tuân thủ 5 công thức phối font thông minh tại [`rules/typography_system.md`](file:///d:/POSTER_PROMPT_SYSTEM_V2/rules/typography_system.md). Tuyệt đối cấm sử dụng một font chữ đơn điệu từ trên xuống dưới. Kết hợp có chủ đích giữa Display Serif, Cursive Brush Script và Chữ kim loại 3D Beveled mạ vàng/bạc để tạo điểm nhấn thị giác bùng nổ.
- **Headline Architecture:** Phân bổ tỷ lệ chữ động (Hero Word phóng đại 2.5x–3x có chất liệu vật lý 3D hoặc đổ bóng).
- **Sub-headline & Copy Hierarchy:** Clean, readable secondary typography with comfortable line-height and generous letter-spacing.
- **Graphic Accents & UI Elements:** Delicate hairline divider rules, minimal metric badges, callout pins, circular percentage chips, pill labels.
- **Advisor Signature Lockup:**
  - Mandatory handwritten mark: **"Tiến"**
  - Integrated contact block: **Dương Quang Tiến • Trưởng nhóm kinh doanh Manulife • 0935 074 268**
  - Blended seamlessly into the negative space with high readability and zero clutter.
