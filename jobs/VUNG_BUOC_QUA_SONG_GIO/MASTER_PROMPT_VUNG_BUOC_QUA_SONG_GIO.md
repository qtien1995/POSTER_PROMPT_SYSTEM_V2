## 2. PRODUCTION MASTER PROMPT (18 UNIVERSAL BLOCKS CONTRACT)

```text
ROLE & OBJECTIVE
Create a masterpiece commercial financial advertising poster and editorial key visual for Manulife Vietnam. The poster visually reassures clients against severe health crises, depicting critical illness protection as an impenetrable bridge of life. The design must communicate unwavering stability, hope, and premium protection instantly within 3 seconds on mobile screens.

CORE MESSAGE
"Bảo vệ vững vàng trước bệnh lý nghiêm trọng - Vững bước qua sóng gió để giữ trọn tương lai xanh."

VISUAL THESIS & BIG IDEA
An awe-inspiring, luminous emerald green suspension bridge—sculpted as an architectural sanctuary—spans seamlessly over an abyss of violent thunderous storms and treacherous jagged cliffs, safely escorting a family from danger into a sunlit paradise of flourishing green hills and endless tomorrow.

HERO VISUAL & METAPHOR
A monumental, majestic suspension bridge constructed from solid architectural emerald jade and deep pine-green stone, featuring illuminated glass-like roadway elements and refined champagne brass structural cables. The bridge serves as a powerful metaphor for comprehensive critical illness insurance: a rock-solid, illuminated passageway of survival over life's darkest medical and financial perils.

SUBJECT + ACTION + RELATIONSHIPS
Positioned at the mid-point of the bridge walking forward toward the bright horizon is an elegant, real-world Vietnamese family:
- A loving father dressed in clean neutral smart-casual attire, gently holding the hand of his joyful 5-year-old daughter.
- A graceful mother walking warmly alongside, gazing ahead with serenity, confidence, and peace of mind.
- Their backs/profiles are subtly angled toward the viewer as they advance forward together, bathed in radiant golden and emerald rim light, physically safe and elevated far above the perilous chasm.

ENVIRONMENT / SCENE
A dramatic visual dichotomy of two contrasting worlds:
- Below & Foreground Chasm: A terrifying, dark, abyssal canyon with wet, jagged basalt rock spires, turbulent churning storm waves, and brooding dark purple-slate thunderclouds.
- Bridge & Distant Horizon: The illuminated emerald bridge creates an untouchable line of safety. In the background ahead, the clouds part to reveal a breathtaking, sun-drenched, optimistic green landscape with rolling hills, flourishing nature, and crystal-clear skies.

COMPOSITION & HIERARCHY
- Aspect Ratio: 3:4 vertical orientation.
- Geometric Grid: Strong diagonal perspective leading the viewer's eye along the emerald bridge from the turbulent lower-left/center toward the triumphant, illuminated upper-right landscape.
- Negative Space Allocation: 40% generous negative space allocated in the stormy upper sky zone for the headline typography, and a calm footer zone for supporting copy and advisor credentials.
- Eye Flow: Top Headline -> Hero Family on Emerald Bridge -> Sunlit Horizon -> Bottom Reassurance Copy & Signature.

STYLE & VISUAL DNA (99% BENCHMARK MATCH)
STYLE_012_MANULIFE_PREMIUM_METAPHOR. Quiet Luxury, cinematic architectural realism, Hollywood matte-painting depth, Apple-keynote visual precision, Swiss editorial graphic hierarchy. Strictly avoid cartoonish 3D plastic toys, video-game neon glows, or cheap stock-photo tropes.

LIGHTING & ATMOSPHERE
Dramatic high-contrast chiaroscuro lighting:
- Cool dramatic 4500K slate blues and deep shadows enveloping the stormy canyon chasm below.
- Warm 3200K golden volumetric god rays (crepuscular rays) breaking through the storm clouds, illuminating the distant horizon and casting specular amber highlights.
- Luminous 5200K emerald internal light radiating softly from within the jade bridge paving stones, casting a soft jade rim light on the family's silhouettes.

PALETTE & COLOR SCIENCE
- Base (60%): Deep Manulife Pine Green (#0B3B24), Basalt Charcoal (#14171A), and Thunderstorm Slate (#1E252B).
- Secondary (30%): Glowing Emerald Jade (#00A758 / #1B6B48) and Warm Sunlight Cream (#FBF8F1).
- Accent (10%): Brushed Champagne Gold (#E5C378) for structural accents and warm solar gleams.

MATERIALITY & TEXTURES
- Bridge: Polished translucent emerald jade with delicate internal mineral veins, honed deep green granite piers, brushed champagne brass suspension fittings.
- Chasm: Wet, coarse, jagged volcanic rock faces with realistic water spray and damp textures.
- Clothing: Natural cotton, linen, and soft knit fabrics with authentic folds, weave textures, and natural draping.

CAMERA OPTICS & LENS SPEC
Shot on Hasselblad H6D-100c medium format camera, 85mm prime lens perspective, f/4.0 aperture for edge-to-edge optical sharpness across the subjects and bridge architecture, subtle natural atmospheric falloff on distant clouds, eye-level hero vantage point.

ON-IMAGE COPY (EXACT VIETNAMESE)

"Vững Bước Qua Sóng Gió"

Subheadline:
"Bảo vệ vững vàng trước bệnh lý nghiêm trọng."

Bottom Supporting Copy:
"Giữ trọn Xanh Tương Lai."

Advisor Identity Line:

Handwritten Signature:
"Tiến"

TYPOGRAPHY & GRAPHIC DESIGN ARCHITECTURE (CHATGPT IMAGES 2.5 STANDARD)
- Formula: Modern Power Duo with Swiss Graphic Precision.
- Top Headline ("Vững Bước Qua Sóng Gió"): Set in bold, contemporary geometric sans-serif (e.g., Neue Haas Grotesk / Futura Bold styling), tracking -0.02em, rendered in pure optic white (#FFFFFF) with delicate ambient drop-shadow separating it cleanly from the dark storm clouds.
- Subheadline ("Bảo vệ vững vàng trước bệnh lý nghiêm trọng."): Positioned directly beneath headline with generous 1.4x line spacing, set in clean, elegant medium-weight sans-serif in ivory-white (#F5F5F5).
- Bottom Copy ("Giữ trọn Xanh Tương Lai."): Centered along the lower band in crisp, tracking-spaced (+0.04em) uppercase or title-case sans-serif.
- Clean Lettering Directive: The text must be immaculate, razor-sharp, and completely unadorned. STRICTLY NO leaf icons, no floral graphics, no decorative vine glyphs, and no clipart sprouts attached to any letters.

SIGNATURE SYSTEM (CONTACT MODE)
- Position: Safely integrated into the lower-left or bottom negative space, completely unobstructed by rocks or characters.
- Components:
  1. A delicate, organic handwritten calligraphic mark: "Tiến" in subtle champagne-gold or emerald ink.
  2. A crisp, single-line professional credit in refined 8pt-style typography: "Dương Quang Tiến • Trưởng nhóm kinh doanh Manulife • 0935 074 268".
- Integration: Perfectly balanced into the artwork's negative space; no heavy solid rectangular box or opaque sticker background.

- "Dương Quang Tiến"
- "Trưởng nhóm kinh doanh"
- "Manulife"
- "0935 074 268"
- "Tiến"

Render exactly and only the ALLOWED_TEXT strings. Do not add any other letters, numbers, pseudo-text, labels, captions, UI text, instruction text, placeholder text, watermark, fake website, fake phone number, or unauthorized brand logo.

NEGATIVE CONSTRAINTS (ANTI-AI CLICHÉS)
- No cartoonish glowing shields, no floating plastic hearts, no childish medical cross icons.
- No deformed fingers, no extra hands or feet, no distorted facial anatomy on family members.
- No green leaves or plant sprouts growing out of the text letters.

- No harsh garish neon saturation or low-budget CGI glow effects.
- No watermarks, no copyright symbols, no stock image badges.

TECHNICAL SPEC & ACCURACY LOCK

Do not invent insurance benefits, financial values, interest rates, conditions, medical claims, legal claims, product features, contact data, or credentials. Render only verified facts and exact Vietnamese copy as defined above.
```
